## Project background

ZeeRa [zR] needs a compact developer landing page inspired by the supplied dark glass profile-card reference. The audience is FiveM and Minecraft communities who need an immediate, memorable introduction and a clear path to connect.

## Product shape

- Static single-page portfolio built with the managed React, TypeScript, and Vite scaffold.
- Uses the supplied portrait as the primary identity asset and the name `ZeeRa [zR]`.
- Presents a cinematic glass-card composition over the supplied Obito animation, with dark charcoal-black accents, ambient motion, live local time, and accessible controls.
- The motion control swaps the animated background to a still frame, and reduced-motion preferences receive the still frame automatically.
- Includes About, Work, and Contact panels revealed from the central profile card without navigation or server-side features.
- Identifies ZeeRa as a FiveM and Minecraft developer and links directly to the supplied Discord invite from the card and contact panel.
- Displays the supplied animated verification badge beside the primary ZeeRa wordmark.
- Layers the supplied animated orbit decoration around the avatar portrait.
- Shows the supplied developer and Discord identity badges beneath the role; the Discord badge opens the invite.
- Uses the supplied blue `zR` artwork as the header logo and home link.
- Uses Josefin Sans consistently across interface typography.
- Adds clickable FiveM and Minecraft badges using the supplied platform artwork; FiveM reveals ESX, QBOX, and QBCORE support, while Minecraft reveals all-platform support.
